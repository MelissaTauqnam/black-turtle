[ ABOUT ]

The Meka
Universe

In the distant future, drivers fight in a world divided into 4 Factions. The MekaVerse is a series of 8,888 generative Mekas with more than a hundred elements inspired from the Japanese Mecha universe.

Each piece is unique and has its own color palette and design research. The objective was to create each Meka in an original way, to prioritize quality above quantity.
